LA MAISON x KLUNKERKRANICH

Sonntag 09. August 2026, ab 11 Uhr

*Ostflügel ab 11 Uhr

*Wohnzimmer ab 11 Uhr

*Club „Hinter den Alpen“ ab 12 Uhr 

LA MAISON x KLUNKERKRANICH

For one day only, La Maison lands on Berlin’s most famous rooftop Klunkerkranich.

RSVP HERE:

https://rausgegangen.de/events/la-maison-x-klunkerkranich-0/

The day starts with coffee and pastries then drifts into drinks, music, sandwiches, art and dancing above the city.

Across the whole rooftop, Tender Mesh brings together art, handmade objects and an exhibition at Hinter den Alpen. Expect live performances, flash tattoos, tarot readings, live drawing and DJ sets across two floors.

Come early, stay late

& let’s fly high like kites.

_*_

RSVP Tickets via Rausgegangen recommended.

www.lamaison.berlin

www.klunkerkranich.org

Preisinformation:

We offer RSVP Tickets for this event – tickets give you „priority boarding“ to Klunkerkranich on the day of the event. The event runs till 4PM and transitions into the Tender Mesh Music event from 4PM till midnight – you can stay, leave and return at any point. First RSVP release is priced at 2€ + fees. www.klunkerkranich.org
