THE FADEAWAYS are a spectacle of high energy, garage, pop, and punk music brilliance, dressed in cool matching ’60s Europop-inspired outfits that scream style as loud as their music. Each show is an adrenaline-fueled experience.
Listen: https://thefadeawaysdwrusa.bandcamp.com/album/pretty-wild

CORAL FUZZ are blending elements of garage pysch and surf pop, creating an upbeat, kaleidoscopic vibe with a touch of melancholy, under the particular spell of Texas based legends, the13th Floor Elevators and contemporaries like the Allah Las.
Listen: https://coralfuzz.bandcamp.com/album/youre-so-high

Johnny Teenager is Berlin’s teenage rock group on the scene. All members of the group are multi-instrumentalists. Expect to hear authentic sixties beat/rock ‘n’ roll with a soulful edge.

„I HATE THE NIGHT TIMES“ is their credo that they pray in every language. The Night Times are a Garage Punk „Slam-Down From The Back Allies of Hollywood“ band! They are being called THEE best Garage Punk band in decades and their live performance captures them at their gritty, hook laden best!
Listen: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wrimwugoIbM
𝐓𝐇𝐄 𝐌𝐀𝐒𝐎𝐍𝐈𝐂𝐒 𝐰𝐢𝐭𝐡 𝐋𝐔𝐃𝐄𝐋𝐋𝐀 𝐁𝐋𝐀𝐂𝐊 (𝐔𝐊)
Born from the Medway scene, The Masonics are drenched in 60s Rhythm and Beat and are going to shake you to your musical core with their Rock n Roll coolness and Punk Rock attitude. Performing with „Headcoatee“: Miss LUDELLA BLACK.
Listen: https://spinoutnuggets.bandcamp.com/album/some-other-guys-ep
𝐓𝐇𝐄 𝐉𝐀𝐂𝐊 𝐂𝐀𝐃𝐄𝐒 (𝐔𝐊)
The band proved on their recent LP ‚Something New‘, that there is more for them than 60s Garage, combining their roots with jangly Indie flavours. Expect a unique combo of Garage Rock with highly addictive melodies. New line up with a fifth Jack Cade!
Listen: https://thejackcades.bandcamp.com/album/something-new
